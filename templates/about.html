About

Pirate Market

The campus fashion marketplace built for Hampton University students. Student designers open storefronts, list their pieces, and sell directly to classmates, with every exchange happening in person on campus or through our wallet.

What you'll find here

Storefronts

Each student brand gets one storefront with its logo, banner, bio and contact details, so you always know who you're buying from.

Listings

Listings show photos, price, available sizes and stock. Items are either in stock and ready now, or pre-order and made after you buy.

Categories & Hot

Browse by category, or check the Hot page to see what students are wishlisting, bagging and ordering right now.

How it works

For shoppers

  1. Browse storefronts, categories and the Hot page.
  2. Add to your bag and pick your size.
  3. Check out with your contact info and a campus meeting spot. No card details are ever entered on the site.
  4. Meet the seller in person to pay and pick up your item.

For student brands

  1. Create your storefront with a logo, banner and bio.
  2. Post listings with photos, prices, sizes and stock.
  3. Manage everything from your My Storefront dashboard.
  4. Hand off orders in person and get paid directly.

Trust & safety

  • Hampton students only

    Accounts require an @hamptonu.edu or @my.hamptonu.edu email address.

  • Meet in public

    Choose a busy campus spot such as the student center or library, and inspect your item before paying.

  • Returns

    If something arrives damaged or isn't as described, submit a return request with your order number.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do I do with my confirmation number?

After checkout you get a confirmation page with your confirmation number. Keep it handy, since the returns form asks for it.

Does Pirate Market take a cut of sales?

No. Payment goes directly from the buyer to the student brand at the meetup.

Can students outside Hampton use it?

Not right now. Limiting accounts to Hampton email addresses keeps every buyer and seller part of the same campus community.

How do I start selling?

Choose Create storefront under Selling at the bottom of any page (or in the menu on your phone). Once your storefront is set up you can post your first listing.
